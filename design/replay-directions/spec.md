# Replay screen — design spec (shared input for all directions)

**What:** FilmRoom's replay page, shown right after a student finishes a 22-question SAT Math practice module. It is the screen recruiters will remember.
**Audience:** a high-school student (16–18) right after a 35-minute effort, often a bit anxious; secondarily a hiring panel at a test-prep company.
**Core message, in order:** (1) your estimated score and how many points you left on the table; (2) *why*: points lost by cause; (3) the AI scouting report (1–2 patterns, 3 habits); (4) every question at a glance; (5) your pacing; (6) any one question in depth: what you did second by second, the trap you fell for, the coach note, and an "Ask about this question" chat.
**Tone:** honest and encouraging, like a good coach watching tape with you. Analytical, never shaming.
**Format:** desktop web page, 1440 wide, scrollable; must collapse to 375.
**Fixture (same in all three):** raw 14/22 → est. 570 (540–600); ~160 points lost: trap 60 (q2, q16, q20), time sink 40 (q11, q15), second-guess 20 (q8), wrong tool 20 (q9), concept gap 20 (q21). Brilliant: q17, q18. Detail shown: q9 (quadratic roots, wrong tool: 2m 30s vs 1m 35s target, Desmos never opened, answered 3 instead of 5).
**Constraints:** no College Board / Bluebook branding; original content; body ≥14px; contrast ≥4.5:1; verdict colours must also carry an icon or letter (not colour alone).
